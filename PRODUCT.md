# Product

## Workfriend.ai

Workfriend.ai is intended to be a credible professional home for practical work in:

- AI transformation and enablement;
- enterprise AI adoption;
- AI governance;
- project and transformation delivery;
- practical AI tools and experiments.

## Product principle

A few useful, working and clearly explained things are better than many half-built experiments.

## Primary objective

Make Workfriend.ai useful as:

- a professional portfolio;
- a demonstration of practical AI capability;
- a catalogue of useful tools and experiments;
- a source of informed thinking about AI, delivery and transformation.

## Current priority

Stabilise and improve the existing site before expanding the product portfolio.
